# SuriStanya App Overview
SuriStanya (from Filipino *suri* = scan, *sustansya* = nutrition) is a screening tool for iron deficiency anemia.
It uses AI to analyze four images: tongue, eye conjunctiva, palm, and nail bed.
The app provides a health score and per-modality findings.
**This is a screening tool only. It is NOT a medical diagnosis. Always consult a doctor for blood tests.**

## How to Use
1. Sign up or sign in.
2. Chat with Ava (the assistant) to record symptoms and risk factors.
3. Capture 4 images using the guided camera.
4. Receive a screening result with an AI-generated visual summary.
5. Review your scan history.

## Important Disclaimer
SuriStanya does not replace professional medical advice. If you are concerned about your health, please see a doctor.
