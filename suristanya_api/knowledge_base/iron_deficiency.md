# Iron Deficiency and Anemia
Anemia means your blood has fewer healthy red blood cells or less hemoglobin than normal.
Common signs include fatigue, pale skin, shortness of breath, dizziness, cold hands and feet.
Causes include iron, B12 or folate deficiency, chronic disease, and blood loss.
A CBC blood test confirms it.

## Iron-Rich Foods
Red meat, liver, chicken, fish, spinach, lentils, chickpeas, beans, tofu, fortified cereals.
Pair with vitamin C (citrus, tomato, bell pepper) to boost absorption.
Avoid tea, coffee, or calcium right after meals — they block iron uptake.

## When to See a Doctor
If symptoms are persistent or worsening, if you have severe fatigue or shortness of breath, or if the scan flags moderate/high severity findings.
A simple CBC blood test can confirm anemia and guide treatment.

## Vitamin C
Vitamin C significantly boosts iron absorption — up to 3x when eaten with iron-rich foods.
Good sources: oranges, lemons, guava, tomatoes, bell peppers, broccoli, strawberries.
